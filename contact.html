Platform Growth Audit

Request a Platform Growth Audit.

The audit establishes where your online revenue is actually constrained — visibility, conversion, economics, retention or operations — and the sequence to clear it. It is an application, not an enquiry form.

What happens next

A senior member of the practice reviews every application. If it is a fit, we return with an initial read on your listings and a proposed scope. If not, we say so and explain why.

What we do not do

No instant calendar, no published price list. Scope follows the diagnosis.

Before you apply

Have your Swiggy and Zomato listing links to hand. The initial read is done against the live storefronts, not a description of them.

Submitted details are used only to assess fit and prepare the initial read. See Privacy.
