The problem

Online revenue is not a listing problem. It is a commercial system.

Twelve variables decide aggregator performance — menu, pricing, discounts, ads, conversion, availability and more. Most restaurants optimise them separately. Foresight manages them as one connected system.

Framework

Five disciplines. One connected system.

Discovery → Conversion → Economics → Repeat → Scale

03

04

Repeat

Keep the demand you paid for: ratings, availability, repeat rate.

05

Scale

Reproduce it at the next outlet or city.
