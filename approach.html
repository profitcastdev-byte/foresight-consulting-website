Approach

A disciplined operating model, not a retainer.

Five stages, a fixed rhythm, named ownership on both sides.

DiagnoseDesignExecuteOptimiseScale

01

Diagnose

Find the binding constraint. It is usually not the one being resourced.

02

Design

The operating plan: what changes, in what sequence, with what expected effect. Every item has an owner before work starts.

03

Execute

Changed in the platform, not recommended in a document.

04

Optimise

Weekly review on evidence. Every adjustment recorded, so results stay explainable.

05

Scale

Proven configurations become playbooks. Growth that cannot be reproduced is not scale.
